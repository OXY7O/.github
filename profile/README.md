## ◈ OXY7

**OXY7** is an Infrastructure Operations Platform designed around one principle:

> **Turn infrastructure signals into controlled operational outcomes.**

OXY7 brings infrastructure visibility, operational context, detection, decision-making, controlled execution, governance, and continuous optimization into one operational system.

## ⟳ The Operational Loop

<div align="center">

### Observe → Relate → Baseline → Interpret → Take Action → Assure → Learn

**↻ Repeat**

</div>

ORBITAL operates as a continuous operational loop.

Every **observation** adds context.
Every **relationship** improves understanding.
Every **baseline** establishes expected behavior.
Every **interpretation** supports a decision.
Every **action** produces evidence.
Every **assurance** establishes control and accountability.
Every **outcome** becomes input for continuous learning.
