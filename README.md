## 🪐 About OXY7O

**OXY7O** is the engineering organization behind **OXY7** — an Infrastructure
Operations Platform built to transform operational signals into context,
controlled execution, and measurable outcomes.

> **Make infrastructure easier to understand, operate, automate, and improve at scale.**

OXY7O brings together the platform, services, integrations, specifications,
automation, and engineering practices that form the **OXY7 ecosystem**.

## 🛰️ ORBITAL

**ORBITAL** is the Seven-Orbit Operational Framework behind OXY7.

<div align="center">

### O · R · B · I · T · A · L

**Observe → Relate → Baseline → Interpret → Take Action → Assure → Learn ↻**

</div>

<br>

| | Orbit | Purpose |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| 👁️ | **Observe** | Capture infrastructure state, telemetry, events, health, and operational signals. |
| 🔗 | **Relate** | Connect resources, dependencies, topology, ownership, and operational context. |
| 📐 | **Baseline** | Establish expected operating conditions and recognize meaningful deviation. |
| 🧠 | **Interpret** | Transform signals and context into operational understanding. |
| ⚡ | **Take Action** | Execute controlled automation, orchestration, remediation, and workflows. |
| 🛡️ | **Assure** | Verify outcomes through policy, governance, evidence, and accountability. |
| ♻️ | **Learn** | Turn outcomes into continuous improvements in reliability and efficiency. |

Every operational cycle generates new evidence.

That evidence returns to **Observe**, creating a continuous loop of
understanding, execution, assurance, and improvement.

<br>

## 🧭 Engineering Principles

> **Clarity over complexity. Evidence before action. Control before automation.**

**Clarity**  
Operational state should be understandable without reconstructing the system manually.

**Evidence**  
Decisions and actions should be grounded in observable state and context.

**Control**  
Automation should have explicit boundaries, ownership, policy, and verification.

**System Thinking**  
Observability, inventory, analytics, automation, governance, and reliability
belong to one operational system.

**Continuous Improvement**  
Every outcome should improve future understanding and execution.
