# OXY7O Community Code of Conduct

This is an organization-wide fallback for participation in OXY7O repositories and their public contribution channels. A repository-local code of conduct, moderation policy, or security policy takes precedence.

## Expected conduct

- Communicate respectfully and constructively, including when disagreeing about technical decisions.
- Focus reviews on reproducible behavior, evidence, and proposed changes rather than personal attributes.
- Welcome good-faith questions and contributions from people with different experience levels.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual-property rights, and repository-specific contribution rules.
- Follow maintainers' instructions for issue scope, review, moderation, and disclosure.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, intimidation, targeted personal attacks, unwanted disclosure of personal information, and persistent disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish credentials, private operational details, exploit instructions targeting live systems, or confidential reports in public channels.

## Reporting and moderation

For non-security conduct concerns, contact the maintainers of the affected repository privately through an established trusted channel. Do not post personal details or allegations in a public issue. If the concern involves a maintainer, seek another authorized organization maintainer where available. No organization-wide confidential reporting mailbox or response-time commitment is asserted by this document.

For vulnerabilities or leaked secrets, **do not use the conduct-report route**. Follow the affected repository's `SECURITY.md` and verified private vulnerability-reporting process; see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Do not assume GitHub private reporting is enabled until verified.

Maintainers may ask participants to revise content, moderate or lock discussions, decline contributions, or restrict participation in channels they administer. They should assess reports proportionately, minimize disclosure, avoid retaliation, and document decisions privately when appropriate. Repository administrators retain final authority under applicable platform rules.

## Scope and maintenance

This policy applies to OXY7O-maintained public contribution spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions where enabled. It does not change any repository's license, create a support SLA, authorize open-source publication, or supersede GitHub's own policies. Changes require the owning repository's review and acceptance.
